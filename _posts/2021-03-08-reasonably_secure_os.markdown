The only way to do that was to abandon OS X and commit to using Linux as my primary OS. What followed was the typical confused distro hopping, until one day I searched the web for "most secure linux distro" and arrived at the [Qubes OS](https://www.qubes-os.org/) homepage. > If you're serious about security, @QubesOS is the best OS available today. It's what I use, and free.

-- [Edward Snowden](https://twitter.com/Snowden/status/781493632293605376)
